## 2025-06-08 - Accessible Collapsible Sidebar Navigation
**Learning:** Collapsible sidebars that reduce navigation items to icon-only buttons require dynamically binding `title` (for visual tooltips) and `aria-label` (for screen readers) based on the collapsed state. Static labels or missing labels cause context loss when the text is hidden. Dark-themed components also need explicit `focus-visible:ring-white` or similar to ensure the focus ring is visible against the dark background.
**Action:** When implementing or modifying collapsible sidebars, ensure the collapse/expand toggle has an `aria-expanded` state. Dynamically set `aria-label` and `title` on the navigation links when they become icon-only. Always verify that focus rings have enough contrast against their background context.
