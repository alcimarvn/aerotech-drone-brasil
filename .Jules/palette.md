## 2026-08-23 - Accessible Icon-Only Links in Dark Themes
**Learning:** When using Radix/Tailwind for icon-only social media links or buttons on dark backgrounds, standard focus outlines often lack contrast and the links lack context for screen readers. Using a combination of `aria-label` (mapped to dynamically assigned `name` attributes for lists), explicit `aria-hidden="true"` on the decorative SVGs, and high-contrast custom focus rings (e.g., `focus-visible:ring-emerald-500 focus-visible:ring-offset-[#0a1a15]`) significantly improves accessibility and keyboard navigation visibility.
**Action:** Always verify that mapped icon arrays include a descriptive `name` property, assign it to `aria-label`, hide the inner icon with `aria-hidden`, and explicitly define high-contrast `focus-visible` states that offset against the specific background color of the container, especially in dark mode.
