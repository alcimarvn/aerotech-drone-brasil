## 2026-03-20 - Icon-Only Action Accessibility\n**Learning:** The dashboard heavily relies on icon-only action buttons (like Notifications, Sidebar Collapse) to save space, but they lack `aria-label` attributes, making them inaccessible to screen readers.\n**Action:** Always ensure that any icon-only `<Button>` or `<button>` includes a descriptive `aria-label` to explain its function to assistive technologies.
