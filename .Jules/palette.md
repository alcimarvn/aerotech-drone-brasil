## 2024-08-30 - Fix Footer Accessibility and Focus States
**Learning:** Icon-only elements (like social links and newsletter submit buttons) often miss `aria-label` properties, which screen readers rely on. In Next.js/Tailwind setups, the focus rings need a specific color contrast to remain accessible on dark footer themes. Missing labels for `input` elements also hurt usability for assistive technologies.
**Action:** When creating footer or link arrays containing icons, ensure the objects include a `name` property to serve as `aria-label`s. Ensure that `<input>` forms specifically have visually hidden labels. Additionally, apply custom dark-theme focus rings (e.g., `focus-visible:ring-emerald-500 focus-visible:ring-offset-[#0a1a15]`) and add `aria-hidden="true"` to purely decorative SVGs within these inputs to prevent redundancy.
