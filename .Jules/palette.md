## 2026-07-27 - Dark Theme Focus Rings & Expandable Menu State\n**Learning:** When adding focus rings to interactive components like sidebars on dark themes, default outlines can disappear due to poor contrast. Also, using `{!isCollapsed && <span>text</span>}` unmounts the text, removing the accessible name from icon-only buttons for screen reader users. And collapsible panels require `aria-expanded` on their toggle button.\n**Action:** Replaced conditional rendering of visible text with conditional `sr-only` classes to ensure screen readers always have context. Added `outline-none focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-white focus-visible:ring-offset-2 focus-visible:ring-offset-slate-900` to force highly visible focus rings on dark themes, and bounded `aria-expanded` for the sidebar collapse state.
